# Vibrant Agency Hub

Build a vibrant, modern agency marketing website inspired by the energy and polish of vaynermedia.com, fabricsocial.com, and socialchain.com. Include a strong hero, a prominent UGC people/talent section, a clear services section, work/process/about/contact areas, and a previous clients section featuring CoFoundersLab, UpFounder, ZeeWork, Miracle Regenerative Center, U-Topia, Astra Healthcare, and Vymune. Use bold contemporary colors, expressive typography, high-quality visual layout, responsive design, and a polished agency feel. Use placeholder imagery/content where needed, but make the site feel complete and ready to customize.
